New Challenge Awaits

Greetings Guardians!

Have you ever felt like the game was too easy? Or maybe you found it too hard to progress?
Have you ever wondered why the difficulty levels change almost nothing in the game?
Well, wonder no more!
I am proud to announce that I've made a complete overhaul of the difficulty system in Fortress Guardian!

The new difficulty system dives deep into the core mechanics of Fortress Guardian. It's clever design ensures that the game is challenging for both new and experienced guardians.
So what's new?

1. Enemy spawns

- More variety, more challenge!: Harder difficulties now include more powerful enemies, increased spawn rates of elite enemies and new patterns
- Boss upgrades: Bosses now have new abilities and attacks on higher difficulties

2. Towers

- Scaling difficulty: Tower damage, range, and speed may now decrease on higher difficulties, requiring more strategic placement
- Repair costs: Repairing towers may now be more expensive & slower on higher difficulties, this can even change on different maps, imagine a cold map where your towers take much longer to repair

A new Kind of Challenge

The revamped difficulty system ensures that each level - from Novice to Fortress Guardian - offers a unique challenge.
The game now defaults to the 'Journeyman' difficulty, which is a balanced experience for most players that want to play the game as i intended.

See you on the battlefield Guardians!

Ondrej Janousek

Lead Developer of Fortress Guardian
